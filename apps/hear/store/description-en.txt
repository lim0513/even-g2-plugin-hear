Live captions on your G2 for when you cannot hear what is being said — BRING YOUR OWN SONIOX API KEY (console.soniox.com, pay-as-you-go). The glasses listen to the people around you, Soniox transcribes in real time, and their words appear on the display within about a second. Turn on translation and the original stays on top, translation below.

REQUIREMENTS
1. No key, no captions. Soniox real-time costs about $0.12 per hour of listening, translation included, billed to your own account. The developer runs no server.
2. Audio is streamed to Soniox while listening. Nothing is recorded or stored.

HOW IT WORKS
- Glasses: long-press = start / pause / resume; double-tap = exit.
- Any spoken language is recognised; translate into 60 languages, or show the original only.
- A new line whenever the speaker changes.
- Stops listening after a quiet period and reconnects when someone speaks, so you only pay while people talk.

Interface in 中文, 日本語 and English.
Free and open source. Support: https://ko-fi.com/lim0513
